Accessibility statement

A website designed to welcome every visitor.

Accessibility is an important part of this portfolio. The pages use clear headings, readable text, descriptive image alternatives, and color combinations selected for contrast. The layout adjusts to smaller screens, and keyboard-friendly navigation helps visitors move through the content with ease.
