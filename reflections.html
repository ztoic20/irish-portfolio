My Reflection in Web Design

Looking back on the lessons, practice, and ideas that shaped my learning journey in Web Design.

My learning journey

Learning to create with purpose.

Throughout the Web Design subject, I learned that creating a website involves more than choosing attractive colors and images. A useful website begins with a clear purpose, an understanding of its audience, and a plan for organizing information. Learning about site maps, wireframes, and content planning helped me see why preparation is important before building pages.

The lessons on HTML and CSS introduced me to the structure and presentation of web content. I also explored web-based platforms and learned how Google Sites can be used to organize pages, add content, and create navigation. The topics on embedding office tools and applying brand guidelines showed me how a website can become more informative while keeping a consistent appearance.

Another important lesson was the need to consider accessibility and mobile users. Clear headings, readable text, descriptive image alternatives, working links, and responsive layouts help visitors use a website more comfortably. Reviewing these details reminded me that design decisions should support the people who visit the site.

Overall, this subject helped me connect creativity with planning, organization, and practical digital skills. This portfolio is a record of the topics I studied and the activities I completed. I hope to keep practicing, accept feedback, and apply what I have learned in future academic and professional tasks.
